Amend Complementary and Integrative Health Approaches for Untangling Prevalent Health Issues

Muhammad Anees, Madeeha Asif, Dr. Muhammad Asfand E Yar · Bahria University
Submitted manuscript
  • Integrated a public symptom–diagnosis dataset with a self-created Complementary and Integrative Health (CIH) dataset built with input from clinicians.
  • Compared six classifiers; Multinomial Naïve Bayes and Gradient Boosting reached the best accuracy (97.62%).
  • Coupled prediction with recommendations and follow-up handling for stable, improved and worsened patients.

Suggestive Strategies for Mitigating Adverse Effects in Drug–Drug Interactions

Madeeha Asif, Muhammad Anees, Dr. Muhammad Asfand E Yar · Bahria University
Submitted manuscript
  • Deep-learning prediction of 65 DDI event types from DrugBank data.
  • Features from SMILES molecular structures, targets, enzymes and pathways, with NLP processing and Jaccard similarity.
  • CNN results: 89.89% accuracy, 87.11% precision, 77.23% recall, 80.72% F1-score.
MS Thesis · Bahria University · 2024

Machine learning for CIH decision support

What I built

A symptom-driven disease prediction system covering 41 diseases and 133 severity-weighted symptoms. After prediction it returns a description, precautions, medications, diet and workout guidance, and adjusts its advice at follow-up visits.

Pipeline: data cleaning, Recursive Feature Elimination with a Random Forest estimator (top 100 features) checked against manual clinical review, label and one-hot encoding, then comparison of six models.

Supervisor: Dr. Muhammad Asfand E Yar.
