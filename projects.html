Spotlight

The AI + XR projects in detail

Meta Quest · VR + AI

MEDVR AI — Medical Training Lab

Lead developer · Unity · Convai · Live on Meta Quest

A VR medical training room where a conversational AI agent guides the trainee by voice, explaining the environment, the equipment and the training workflow.

  • Integrated a Convai conversational agent that talks with the player in real time
  • Physics-based interaction with medical instruments, surgical bed, surgical light and in-world UI
Research angle: conversational agents as adaptive tutors in immersive clinical training.
View on Meta Quest
WebXR · Multiplayer + AI

Archimedes — Multiplayer Science Lab

Lead developer · WebXR · Multiplayer · Convai

A browser-based multiplayer learning world where an AI-driven Archimedes character talks with players and leads them through hands-on physics experiments.

  • Shared multiplayer sessions where players run several experiments together
  • Archimedes’ Screw: the AI character assembles it first, then players assemble the parts and turn the handle to lift water
Research angle: AI characters and collaborative play for learning in shared virtual spaces.
Play on itch.io
VR · Sports Science

Brains Boots & Beyond VR

Lead developer · Unity · Featured on national TV

Decision-training VR for athletes: players step onto a game-realistic virtual field, read the play, decide under pressure and see the outcome. Built for Australia’s National Science Week through a partnership of Concinnity, the University of Southern Queensland (UniSQ) and the Western Clydesdales.

  • Immersive field environment, avatar systems and decision-logic scenarios, deployed on headsets
  • Training the mental side of sport that physical drills cannot reach
Research angle: measuring decision-making and cognitive load under pressure in VR.
